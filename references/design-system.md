# Design system

- Canvas: #F7F9FC; cards: #FFFFFF; accent cards: #EDF4FF.
- Primary text: #142338; secondary text: #526276.
- Border: #DDE5EF; accent border: #A8C8F4.
- Accent: #3776D6. Connectors: #91A4BA.
- Use restrained contrast, consistent spacing and rounded cards.
- Keep titles concise; move supporting details to subtitles.
- Prefer hierarchy over decoration. Avoid gradients and unnecessary shadows.
